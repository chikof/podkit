Skeleton

Motion spec

Fluid and weighty. 180 / 320 / 560 ms, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Hover lifts a pane 3 px and brightens its edge light; dialogs scale from 0.94 and blur-in over a blurred backdrop. Page change is a 420 ms blur-fade. Backdrop blur is the only blur: it signals elevation, never decoration.

Cross-document view transitions (@view-transition) run when served over http (e.g. python3 -m http.server); browsers treat file:// pages as separate origins and skip them. Everything else works from file://. All motion is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
